Ask your question ↗
What is the BLive CHARGE model?

A franchisee-owned, company-operated model: you invest in EV charging infrastructure while BLive manages setup and operations under the agreed scope.

How do I earn?

Income is utilisation-based. Actual payouts depend on charging activity, applicable costs and the commercial terms agreed for your project.

Can I suggest a location?

Share the site with the team for assessment. Demand, vehicle access, parking and power availability are among the factors that affect suitability.

How much will my project cost?

Request a site-specific proposal covering equipment, installation, inclusions, exclusions and ongoing costs. Configuration and site requirements affect the total.

What should I review before signing?

Review ownership, site arrangements, cost allocation, service responsibilities, payout terms, utilisation assumptions and exit provisions in the agreement.
